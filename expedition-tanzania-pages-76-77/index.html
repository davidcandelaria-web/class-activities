3 · Read: A journey with new perspectives

On an expedition in Tanzania, a student group spent time with local hosts and learned about the community around them. The travelers discovered that a meaningful trip can involve more than sightseeing. They exchanged stories, shared activities, and noticed how daily routines can differ from their own. Some moments went differently from what they expected. By listening and asking respectful questions, they learned to reconsider their assumptions. After returning home, the students could describe what they had learned about people and about themselves.
