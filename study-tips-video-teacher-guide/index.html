3 Tips on How to Study Effectively

Full 30-question activity and answer key.

Teacher Overview

Video: TED-Ed — 3 tips on how to study effectively (about 5 minutes).

Main ideas: active retrieval/self-testing, interleaving topics, and spacing study over multiple days. The activity also checks basic ideas about memory, sleep, mistakes, and the surgical-resident example.

Student format: 10-second minimum for newly reached questions, no visible timer, Previous/Next, question navigator, editable answers before Finish, autosave, Grade and Download.

Teacher link: keep separate from students because all answers are visible.
