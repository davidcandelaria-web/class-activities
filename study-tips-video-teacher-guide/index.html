6 Awesome Study Challenges!

Quick 6th-grade version for a 30-minute class — complete answer key.

Teacher Overview

30-minute plan: Play the video, briefly ask students which tip surprised them, then let them complete these six quick challenges.

Focus: sleep and memory, self-testing, learning from mistakes, mixing topics, and spacing study instead of cramming.

Student format: six multiple-choice challenges, Previous/Next, editable answers before Finish, autosave, Grade and Download.

Keep this teacher link separate from students because all answers are visible.

Challenge 1Brain Power! 🧠

Sleep helps!

After you study, what can help your brain keep what you learned?
  1. getting a good night's sleep ✓ CORRECT
  2. staying awake all night
  3. never taking a break
Answer: getting a good night's sleep
Challenge 2Challenge Yourself! 🎯

No peeking!

You want to know if you REALLY remember something. What should you do?
  1. quiz yourself before looking at the answer ✓ CORRECT
  2. read the answer while guessing
  3. copy the same sentence again and again
Answer: quiz yourself before looking at the answer
Challenge 3Mistake Power! 💥

Oops = learning

You get a flashcard WRONG. What is the smartest next move?
  1. check the answer and learn from the mistake ✓ CORRECT
  2. throw the flashcard away
  3. give up studying forever
Answer: check the answer and learn from the mistake
Challenge 4Mix It Up! 🔀

Brain shuffle

Which study session best uses the video's idea of mixing topics?
  1. a little vocabulary, grammar, and reading ✓ CORRECT
  2. the exact same word 100 times
  3. staring at one page without practicing
Answer: a little vocabulary, grammar, and reading
Challenge 5Don't Cram! 📚

Future-you says thanks

You have a quiz on Friday. Which plan is best?
  1. review a little on several days ✓ CORRECT
  2. wait until very late Thursday night
  3. start studying after the quiz
Answer: review a little on several days
Challenge 6Final Boss! 🏆

Three super study moves

Which combination best matches the video's advice?
  1. test yourself, mix topics, and spread out practice ✓ CORRECT
  2. cram, skip sleep, and never check mistakes
  3. highlight everything and hope for the best
Answer: test yourself, mix topics, and spread out practice
