Before you begin

First, copy the Reading Skill box from the board into your notebook. Use the rule and examples in your notebook to answer the questions below. Each question also gives you the sentence you need.

  1. Read the example inside each question. Find the pronoun, then look for the noun it replaces.
  2. Ask: Who or what does this pronoun replace? Give the noun, not another pronoun.
  3. For typed answers, enter a short noun phrase. Spelling and capitalization do not matter, but make your meaning clear.
  4. Complete each question in order. Your answer saves on this computer under your name and section.
  5. At the end, choose Finish, then Grade, then Download. Give the downloaded report to your teacher.
