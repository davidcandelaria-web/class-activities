PART 1 · AI OR ME? · 5 MIN

Who should do the work?

Choose AI, ME, or BOTH. There is no single correct answer—be ready to defend your choices.

Discuss: Where should the line be between AI helping you and AI replacing you?

PART 2 · BUT DID YOU ACTUALLY LEARN IT? · 8 MIN

95% with AI vs. 80% by yourself

Scenario A: A university student asks AI to write an entire 1,500-word essay and receives 95%.

Scenario B: A friend writes the essay without AI. It takes four hours and receives 80%.

PART 3 · CHEATING, SMART, OR IT DEPENDS? · 8 MIN

Where is the line?

Choose Cheating, Smart Use, or It Depends. Compare your answers with a classmate and try to find at least one disagreement.

PART 4 · THE BIG QUESTION · 7 MIN

University announces: “Students may use AI for ALL homework.”

Choose the statement closest to your opinion.

Talk about it

What is homework supposed to do? If AI can finish an assignment in 30 seconds, is the assignment still useful? Should professors change homework instead of banning AI? What skills could students lose—or gain?

FINAL CHALLENGE

🤖 AI Allowed… But Can You Explain It?

Your professor says: “You can use as much AI as you want on your homework. Tomorrow, I will choose one question and you must explain your answer—with no phone and no AI.”

Would this be a fair university rule?
If you knew the professor could question you tomorrow, would you still let AI do everything?
